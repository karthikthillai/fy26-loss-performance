2. Context
Current state and contributing factors

The Payments loss rate is elevated at 10.3 bps YTD compared to the target of 8.5 bps, resulting in a 2.1 bps ($32M) variance for the year. At current trends, FY’26 will be $40M unfavorable. Similarly, while Bill Pay YTD loss rates are within targets, the segment experienced a critical spike in April due to Account Takeover (ATO) fraud, severely impacting unit economics and leading to unprofitability in Q3. Specifically, Bill Pay revenue in April was $3M, but losses reached $7.8M.

⚠ Bill Pay — April Alert
ATO fraud resulted in $3M in losses attributable to Out-of-SLA (OOSLA) cases — $1.5M in write-offs and $1.5M in negative balances. Revenue of $3M vs. losses of $7.8M created negative unit economics in Q3.

Onboarding 2.0 is being ramped to 90% of payment applications by May 8th, projected to secure approximately $2M in potential loss savings. Risk also recommends suspending the capability to open a banking second entitlement account, as this enables fraudsters to access faster funding and an easy exit method. This onboarding flow lacks key controls, and we prefer to develop it correctly within Onboarding 2.0. We do not recommend changing liability on ATO losses due to the high historical OOP rates, but it remains a viable lever if necessary.
